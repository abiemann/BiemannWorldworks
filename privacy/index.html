Velvet Sky privacy policy

Biemann Worldworks makes Velvet Sky for Meta Quest. Your headset calculates the night sky, and your observing location stays on the headset. Velvet Sky does not create a separate account for you or require an internet connection to show the sky.

Your observing location

You can choose a city, enter a named place with latitude, longitude and time zone, or scan a location QR code. The app uses that observing place and your chosen date and time to calculate the stars, Moon and planets. Velvet Sky does not request the headset's device-location permission or automatically obtain your physical location.

The app keeps your most recent named personal place, including its coordinates and time zone, in its private storage. Selecting a city does not delete that saved personal place. It stays there until you replace it with another saved place or remove the app's stored data. The app does not keep a location history, track your location in the background, or send your coordinates to Biemann Worldworks.

Location QR codes and headset camera

The location QR generator asks for browser location permission only when you choose Use my location. You can also enter coordinates yourself and choose the time zone at that place. Your chosen name, coordinates and time zone are encoded into a QR code entirely in your browser. They are not included in the page address or uploaded to us. The generator keeps them only in the open page unless you download the QR image. Anyone who can scan that image can read its location, so share it only as you intend.

When you choose Scan QR in Velvet Sky, the app requests headset camera permission. Camera images are processed on the headset to read the code. They are not saved or uploaded. Camera access stops when scanning finishes, is cancelled, or the app loses focus. A valid scan fills the GPS form for review; only Save applies and stores that location. You can revoke camera permission in the headset's app settings and continue entering coordinates manually.

What else stays on the headset

Velvet Sky saves your observing and comfort preferences, including scenery, posture, sky-display options, sound, selected time zone and time-playback settings. It processes headset and controller positions and button input to draw your view, align the sky and let you use the menus. It does not record a movement history.

The app does not request internet or microphone permission. It has no ads, analytics or crash-reporting service that sends information to Biemann Worldworks. We do not receive, sell or share your in-app location, camera images, movements or preferences.

Children and families

Velvet Sky is intended for people aged 10 and older, including children aged 10–12, teens and adults. The local processing and storage described above apply to children as well as adults. Choosing a city lets you see its sky without entering your own physical location. A place label does not need to include a child's real name or home address.

The optional website QR generator does not ask for a date of birth or create an account. A parent or guardian should help children with browser location permission and deciding whether to download or share a location QR image. Parents and guardians can use the contact below for privacy or deletion questions. You do not need to send us a child's coordinates or date of birth to ask for help.

How to delete your saved data

Use the headset's app settings to clear Velvet Sky's app data, or uninstall Velvet Sky. This removes the app's saved personal place, coordinates and preferences from the headset. Velvet Sky excludes its private storage from cloud backup and device transfer. You can also replace a saved personal place through A → Location → Enter GPS → Save.

The QR generator does not save your location in cookies or browser storage. Clear the form or close the page when you finish; your browser controls any history or form restoration. If you downloaded or shared a QR image, delete that image from your downloads and any copies you made or shared. Revoking browser location permission or headset camera permission stops future access but does not delete a location already saved in the app or in a downloaded QR image.

Using this website and the Meta store

The optional is.gd/vtsky shortcut redirects to the location generator with detailed statistics enabled. Its public statistics show accesses to the shortcut, which can include repeat visits and automated requests. is.gd may collect browser, country and referring-page information and retain IP addresses for abuse prevention under its privacy policy. Your place name and coordinates are not included in the shortcut or sent to is.gd by the generator. You can use the direct generator address to avoid the shortcut service.

GitHub Pages hosts this site and its trailer. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. You can read about this in GitHub’s Pages documentation and GitHub’s privacy statement. Meta handles information about your use of its store, account and headset services under Meta’s privacy policy.
