Velvet Sky privacy policy

Biemann Worldworks makes Velvet Sky for Meta Quest. Your headset calculates the night sky, and your observing location stays on the headset. You can use the app without an account or location permission.

Your observing location

You can choose a preset place, type coordinates, or ask Velvet Sky to use the headset’s location. If you choose the headset option and grant permission, the app requests a location while you are using it. Velvet Sky uses the latitude, longitude and observing time to place the stars and Moon in your sky.

The app remembers your most recent personal observing coordinates in its private storage. It does not keep a location history, track your location in the background, or send your coordinates to Biemann Worldworks.

Location QR codes and headset camera

The location QR generator asks for browser location permission only when you choose Use my location. You can also enter coordinates yourself and choose the time zone at that place. Your chosen name, coordinates and time zone are encoded into a QR code entirely in your browser. They are not included in the page address or uploaded to us. The generator keeps them only in the open page unless you download the QR image. Anyone who can scan that image can read its location, so share it only as you intend.

When you choose Scan QR in Velvet Sky, the app requests headset camera permission. Camera images are processed on the headset to read the code. They are not saved or uploaded. Camera access stops when scanning finishes, is cancelled, or the app loses focus. A valid scan fills the GPS form for review; only Save applies and stores that location. You can revoke camera permission in the headset's app settings and continue entering coordinates manually.

What else stays on the headset

Velvet Sky saves your observing and comfort preferences, including scenery, posture and sound. It uses headset and controller input to draw your view and let you use the menus. The app has no ads, analytics or crash-reporting service. Biemann Worldworks does not receive, sell or share your in-app location, movements or preferences.

How to delete your saved data

Open the Velvet Sky pause menu, choose Settings, then Reset to defaults. This removes your saved personal coordinates and resets the app’s preferences. Clearing Velvet Sky’s app data or uninstalling the app also removes its private storage. Velvet Sky excludes that storage from cloud backup and device transfer.

You can turn off future access to the headset’s location in the headset’s app permissions. Turning permission off or choosing a preset place keeps your previously saved coordinates until you reset the app or clear its data.

Using this website and the Meta store

The optional is.gd/vtsky shortcut redirects to the location generator with detailed statistics enabled. Its public statistics show accesses to the shortcut, which can include repeat visits and automated requests. is.gd may collect browser, country and referring-page information and retain IP addresses for abuse prevention under its privacy policy. Your place name and coordinates are not included in the shortcut or sent to is.gd by the generator. You can use the direct generator address to avoid the shortcut service.

GitHub Pages hosts this site and its trailer. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. You can read about this in GitHub’s Pages documentation and GitHub’s privacy statement. Meta handles information about your use of its store, account and headset services under Meta’s privacy policy.
